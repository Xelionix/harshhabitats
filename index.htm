Harsh Habitats

Build, Battle, Barter, and Thrive throughout the vast world of Harsh Habitats

About the Game

Harsh Habitats is an interactive multi-player and single-player experience, where one is placed on land and forges a civilization. Lead the next war pact, build an economic powerhouse, develop agriculture giant, or thrive as a peaceful band of gem miners. Experiment with modes like Pirates and Duels and challenge your friends! Be ready to defend your village—or invade others’—and Build, Battle, Barter, and Thrive throughout the vast world of Harsh Habitats!

System Requirements

  • Minimum: Intel Core i3 or equivalent, 8GB RAM, NVIDIA GTX 660 / AMD Radeon HD 7850, DirectX 11 compatible, 30 FPS minimum with low scalability settings.
  • Recommended: Intel Core i7 or equivalent, 16GB RAM, NVIDIA GTX 1070 / AMD Radeon RX 580, SSD storage, 60 FPS gameplay.
